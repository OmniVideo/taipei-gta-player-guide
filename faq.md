# FAQ

Answers mirror the FAQ on [https://taipeigta.com/](https://taipeigta.com/) (EN: `/en`). Mission details always follow the live build.

### What is Taipei GTA?

A Taipei-themed open-world browser game with walking, scooters/cars, map navigation, and missions. Start at the top of the homepage; controls and guides are below.

### Do I need to download or register?

No game install and no registration on this site. Select **Start game**, wait for resources, then use the main menu. First load needs a network download — not an offline title.

### Is it free?

Homepage play entry and guides are free. Mission content and any service details follow the current version.

### Phone or controller?

Touch, keyboard/mouse, and controller modes exist. Phones use virtual stick + buttons (landscape recommended). Performance depends on device and browser. Controllers: use the in-game controller page.

### How do I ride, open the map, and take missions?

`F` enter/exit · `W` throttle · `S` brake/reverse · `A`/`D` steer · `Space` handbrake · `M` map · `T` phone for missions and more.

### Black screen, long load, or no input?

Wait for progress; check network; click the play area; close extra tabs; use **Reload game** once; see `troubleshooting-browser-performance.md`.

### Does sharing include my save?

Share buttons only share the page URL — not scores or saves. Different browsers, devices, or private mode can use separate storage.

### Is this official Rockstar GTA?

**No.** Taipei GTA is an independent browser game with **no official affiliation** with Rockstar Games or the Grand Theft Auto series. Homepage art is AI-generated themed illustration, not a gameplay screenshot.
