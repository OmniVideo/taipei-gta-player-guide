# Scooter driving tips

From the live “scooter riding” section on [taipeigta.com](https://taipeigta.com/). Goal: **control first, speed later**. Tips apply inside the game only.

## Basics

1. Check camera angle and which way the scooter faces before holding throttle.
2. Use short `W` presses to learn acceleration.
3. Brake with `S` **before** the turn when possible — recovering mid-corner is harder.
4. `A` / `D` steering: look ahead early at junctions.

## Practice on a straight road

- Start → slow → stop → accelerate again.
- `S` also reverses; release it when stopped unless you want to go backward.

## Let the camera help

- If the body blocks the road, try `V` for another camera.
- Slow down at unfamiliar junctions instead of making large repeated corrections.

## Navigation is a hint

- Set a destination with `M`, then watch the road.
- Stop when you need to re-check the route rather than staring only at the nav cue.

## Handbrake / drift later

- `Space` while driving is handbrake.
- Learn normal turns first; leave extra stopping distance in rain (in-game).

## Scooter vs car

Scooters suit short scenic trips. Try a car when you want a different view or steering feel. Fastest arrival is not the only open-world goal.
