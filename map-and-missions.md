# Map and missions overview

Condensed from [https://taipeigta.com/guides/map-missions](https://taipeigta.com/guides/map-missions). Not a full spoiler guide or completion tracker.

## Map (`M`)

1. Focus the game, press `M`.
2. Find yourself and your district first.
3. Use legends to filter (missions, metro, shops, hospital/police, temples, night markets, landmarks — counts change with updates).
4. Zoom the target, click to set **one** navigation point, close the map, then drive/walk.
5. If you pan too far, recenter with `C` / “return to me”.

Do not assume real Taipei walking times or MRT exits match the game city.

## Phone (`T`) → missions

1. Open the in-game phone (not your real phone).
2. Open Missions; categories may include nearby, main, side, part-time, racing, culture, and filters by area.
3. Locked missions are marked separately — start with what is available.
4. Read region, goals, and conditions; accept / set nav for **one** job at a time.
5. Map marker for a start (site text: 「任」): enter the glow and press `E` (touch: Interact).
6. Finishing a step may need dialogue, another location, or a vehicle change — re-read the prompt.

## Stuck near the marker?

1. Is the mission accepted, locked, or already done?
2. Nav may drop you nearby — walk into the actual interact zone.
3. Close phone/map, click the play area, press `E` / Interact.
4. Re-read conditions (talk, swap vehicle, prerequisite).

Avoid endless page reloads as the only fix; note mission name, area, and last successful step instead.

## Version caveats

- Older articles may quote mission counts (e.g. “93”) from a past date — trust the live mission UI.
- Requested features (e.g. underground maze ideas) are not automatically “in game”; check the current map.
- Progress does not travel with a shared URL; keep the same browser/device when possible.
