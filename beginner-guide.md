# Beginner guide — first session

Practical order from the [Taipei GTA](https://taipeigta.com/) homepage. These are starter suggestions, **not** an official mission sequence.

## 1. Load and pick controls

1. Open the homepage and select **Start game**.
2. Wait for the in-game loading progress to finish.
3. In the main menu, skim character options, settings, and Controls (keyboard / touch / controller).
4. PC: keyboard + mouse. Phone: touch (landscape recommended). Controller: confirm the browser sees the pad, then read the in-game button page.

## 2. Walk before you ride

1. Click the play area so WASD reaches the game.
2. Move with `W A S D`, look with the mouse.
3. Approach a scooter or car and press `F`.
4. On a clear road, practice throttle and brake. Remember: `Space` jumps on foot and handbrakes while driving.

## 3. Map a nearby destination

1. Press `M`, pick somewhere **close**, set navigation.
2. Short trips teach reading roads, turning, and stopping better than racing across the whole city.
3. After arriving, stop, look around on foot, then choose the next stop.

## 4. Check the in-game phone

1. Press `T` for missions, map helpers, metro, and related features.
2. Read the mission text before traveling.
3. Availability, completion rules, and rewards follow what the game shows now.

## Suggested first 15 minutes

Walk one block → enter a scooter → one short guided trip → open phone and read (don’t rush) one unlocked mission → park and explore on foot near a landmark.
