# City exploration notes

Taipei GTA builds a **creative** city inspired by Taipei landmarks. Use these notes to plan a session; always trust the in-game map for streets and distances.

## Landmark themes (site suggestions)

| Theme | Places often mentioned | How to play it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Streets & detail | Ximending, Taipei Main Station area | Walk first, then a short ride |
| Skyline | Xinyi, Taipei 101 | Set map nav, practice braking, get out to look |
| Temples & night markets | Longshan Temple, Shilin / Raohe-style night market cues | Filter map legends; one stop at a time |

All brand names in scenery are **fictional parodies** per the site.

## Session ideas

1. **First visit** — Walk one block, set a nearby pin, ride there, exit vehicle, look around.
2. **Theme night** — Only night markets, or only skyline, instead of collecting every pin.
3. **Landmark compass** — Use Taipei 101 as a visual anchor, then relate it to the `M` map.

## Metro as another travel option

Press `T` and check metro-related entries. The game indicates metro entrances can move you between areas — follow in-game directions rather than real-world exit maps.
